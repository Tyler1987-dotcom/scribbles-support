Scribbles: Write & Share

A simple notes app for writing things down, crossing them out, and sharing your Scribbles.

Privacy Policy

Scribbles is designed to keep your information on your device.

Information Stored by Scribbles

Scribbles allows you to create notes, add lines, cross out completed lines, and associate a contact with a Scribble. This information is stored locally on your iPhone.

Contacts

Scribbles can use Apple's Contacts picker when you choose to add a contact to a Scribble. Scribbles only receives the contact information you select through Apple's contact picker.

Selected contact information is stored locally on your device as part of the associated Scribble.

Sharing

When you choose to share a Scribble, the app uses the standard iOS sharing system. Information is only shared when you intentionally choose to use the Share function.

Data Collection

Scribbles does not require an account and does not operate its own server for storing your Scribbles. The app does not use advertising or subscription services.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This privacy policy may be updated if the features or practices of Scribbles change.

Contact

For questions about Scribbles or this privacy policy, please use the Contact Support button above.
